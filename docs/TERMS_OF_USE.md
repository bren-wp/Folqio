# Folqio Terms of Use

**Effective date:** October 13, 2026  
**Created by:** Brendigo

Folqio is a PDF reading, scanning, organizing and export application. Use it only with documents you own or are authorized to access, process and share.

## Document responsibility

Keep a backup of important originals and review files after modification or export. Some Android PDF page operations produce image-based copies that do not preserve searchable text, forms, accessibility tagging or digital signatures. Folqio is not a validated irreversible redaction, legal certification or cryptographic signature system.
