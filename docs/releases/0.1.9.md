# Folqio v0.1.9 — safer PDF annotations

Folqio helps you keep a dependable original while making practical edits on iPhone and iPad.

### What's new
- **Add text to a PDF** using a free-text annotation overlay. This adds new text; it does not modify existing characters in the original PDF.
- **Save highlights and notes separately.** Folqio writes an annotated copy to your protected local document library, keeping the source PDF unchanged.
- **A calmer editing flow.** Enter notes and text overlays in a dedicated editor, with clear warnings that edited copies of signed PDFs may no longer have valid signatures.
- **Know your version.** Android and iOS Settings display the app's installed version alongside Brendigo authorship.
- **More safeguards.** Annotation copy tests confirm that source file bytes remain unchanged; password-protected files are not silently rewritten without encryption.

### Platform availability
Android: PDF reading, local documents, camera/photo input, page operations (rasterized), sharing and ZIP export.

iOS: PDFKit reading, selectable-text search, free-text annotations, notes and highlights in separate copies, document scanning, on-device OCR, PDF page operations, and ZIP export.

### Important limitations
This version is a **development prerelease**. Android's page-copy operations rasterize PDF pages and do not preserve selectable text, forms or digital signatures. iOS free-text annotations are not full replacement/editing of existing PDF text. Redaction, signed distribution, comprehensive visual/device certification and full translations remain unfinished.

Privacy first: Folqio does not require user accounts or an advertising SDK, and document processing stays on the device unless you explicitly share it.
